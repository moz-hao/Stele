Know the responsibility

Self-custody puts access in your hands

Trust Wallet is designed as a self-custody wallet. Instead of relying on an account provider to authorize every transfer, the wallet holder controls the credentials used to approve blockchain transactions.

This model offers direct control, but it also makes backup and verification essential. A lost recovery phrase may prevent wallet recovery, while a phrase exposed to someone else can place every related asset at risk.

No legitimate support representative should ask for your recovery phrase or private keys.

Wallet security checklist

  • Keep the recovery phrase and private keys offline and out of screenshots, email and cloud notes.
  • Verify the full website domain before connecting a wallet or approving a signature.
  • Read transaction details, token allowances and network fees before confirming.
  • Use a device lock and keep the operating system and wallet software updated.
  • Remove unused decentralized app connections and review active token approvals.
  • Assume unsolicited support messages, urgent giveaways and recovery requests are scams.
